⚠ Data Quality Note

Bookings marked "Non Refund" show a — cancellation rate — nearly all of them. This is not a signal that non-refundable deposits cause cancellations (guests forfeiting money makes no business sense as a driver). It's a known artifact in how this dataset's reservation_status field was recorded. Worth flagging as a reminder to sanity-check counter-intuitive findings against how the underlying data was actually collected — a number can be technically correct and still misleading.

METHODOLOGY

Built by loading the raw dataset into SQLite and answering each business question with SQL (heavy use of GROUP BY, aggregate functions, and CASE WHEN bucketing), then visualizing results interactively here. Dataset: 119,389 bookings from a city hotel (Lisbon) and resort hotel (Algarve), July 2015–August 2017 — Antonio, Almeida & Nunes (2019), Data in Brief. Original prices in EUR, converted to CAD at 1 EUR = 1.61 CAD (Aug 2026 rate) for a Canadian audience.
